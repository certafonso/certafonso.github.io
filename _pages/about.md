## About Me:
I’m a Ph.D. student at Instituto Superior Técnico and a researcher at ISR/LARSyS, focusing on robot task planning with Large Language Models (LLMs). My research aims to enable robots to understand and act on natural language commands, making interactions between humans and robots more intuitive and flexible. This involves combining speech recognition, semantic mapping, and advanced planning techniques to build more capable robots for real-world applications.

I’m particularly interested in developing systems that allow robots to process and act on spoken language in a seamless way, as this is critical for robots to operate effectively in dynamic home environments. My current work focuses on leveraging LLMs for task planning, replacing traditional rule-based systems with more adaptive, context-aware models that can handle complex and ambiguous instructions.
